Lytics announced it has been acquired by Contentstack, a leader in headless Content Management Systems (CMS) and Composable Digital Experience Platforms (DXP). Together the companies aim to deliver the first fully integrated composable DXP, combining real-time customer data with personalized content to help brands create impactful digital experiences at scale.
